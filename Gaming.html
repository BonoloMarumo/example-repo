What is Gaming?

Gaming is a recreational activity that involves playing video games on various platforms like consoles, computers, and smartphones. It spans genres like action, strategy, and simulation.

Who Invented Video Games?

Ralph H. Baer invented the first video game console, the Magnavox Odyssey, in the early 1970s.

Fun Fact: The first commercially successful arcade game was Pong by Atari, released in 1972.
